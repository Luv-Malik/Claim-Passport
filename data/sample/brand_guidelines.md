# Lumio Invoice Brand Guidelines v3
This is a SAMPLE guideline file for the demo brand "Lumio Invoice" (a fictional invoicing app).
Rule lines start with a code in square brackets. See docs/GUIDELINE_FORMAT.md.

## Tone
- [T-1] Keep a professional, friendly voice. No slang. avoid: "awesome", "gonna", "wanna", "super cool", "kinda", "stuff" use: "great", "going to", "want to", "impressive", "somewhat", "things" severity: low ref: Brand Guidelines v3, section 1.1 (Voice)
- [T-2] Use at most one exclamation mark in any piece. severity: low
- [T-3] Do not write words in ALL CAPS. severity: low

## Terminology
- [TERM-2] Our product is always written "Lumio Invoice" (two words, capital L and I). This is checked through the glossary. severity: medium ref: Brand Guidelines v3, section 2.1 (Product name)
- [TERM-3] Say "customers", not "clients". This is checked through the glossary. severity: low ref: Brand Guidelines v3, section 2.2 (Words we use)

## Messaging
- [M-1] Prices and offers must say "Terms apply". severity: medium
- [M-2] Never promise legal or tax outcomes. severity: high lock: yes
- [M-3] Do not call anything "cheap": say "affordable". This is checked through the glossary. severity: low ref: Brand Guidelines v3, section 3.3 (Price words)

## Accessibility
- [A-1] Keep sentences short (under 28 words).
- [A-2] Every image needs alt text.
- [A-4] Text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1. severity: high

## Visual
- [V-1] Use only brand colours: Navy #17202A, Orange #C75B0B, Teal #127C7A, Blue #2F6DB5, Cream #F4F2EC.
- [V-2] Use only the brand fonts: Inter and Georgia.

## Claims
- [C-1] Bold claims ("best", "#1", "100% secure", "24x7") need an approved source.
- [C-2] Numbers must match the approved fact. severity: high
- [C-5] Comparisons with other brands need Legal review. severity: high lock: yes
